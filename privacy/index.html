Privacy

What we keep

The short version: we count page loads, the game keeps what it needs to work, and anything more is your choice.

Page counts

When a page loads, we add one to a daily total for that page and a broad source, such as WhatsApp, Instagram or search. These counts are page loads, not people. Reloads and repeat visits count again. They are not linked to your game, and we do not use cookies or browser storage for these counts.

Your game

To play, your browser keeps a random session key and your progress, such as notes and clues you have found. Our server keeps a matching record so the game can work. It contains your progress, status, answer-check counts and gameplay timestamps. It does not store your name. It records whether each clue check was right or wrong and, for The Hidden Drop, the places you found. It never stores a wrong answer.

Your progress can be restored for 24 hours. After that, the case starts fresh.

Optional analytics

If you tap Allow analytics, we also record when you start a case, whether each answer was right or wrong, and how long you took. This is linked to a separate random ID. If you say no, the game works exactly the same. You can change your choice any time from Privacy & analytics at the bottom of each case.

Hosting

We do not store IP addresses in these records. Our hosting providers, GitHub and Supabase, process request details, including IP addresses, under their own privacy policies. Fonts are served from this site.
