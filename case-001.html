Clues & Evidence
Clue 01 — Camera record

The security camera on the third floor shows Reena Sood entering the partners' office alone at 7:48 pm and leaving at 7:51 pm. No other person entered the office between 7:30 pm and the 8:05 pm alarm.

article>
Clue 02 — Turnstile record

Priya Nairstrong> left the building at 7:55 pmstrong> — ten minutes before the fire alarm. Her exit is confirmed by the ground-floor turnstile log. She did not re-enter.

article>
Clue 03 — Keycard log

The building's keycard log records Reena Sood entering the mail room at 7:45 pm. Her statement says she did not go to the mail room. The keycard does not lie.

article>
Clue 04 — Replica discovery

The envelope on the desk was not missing — it was still present the next morning. It was a replicastrong>, assembled from the same Crane Crest Ivory stock as the original. The substitution went undetected until the contents were examined.

article>
Clue 05 — Torn sleeve

A torn paper sleeve with the same paper-stock code as the replica was found in the mail-room bin. Forensics confirmed it was trimmed with the cutter kept in that room.

article>
Clue 06 — Printer log

The colour printer on the third floor logged one job at 7:42 pm: one copy, "Acquisition Summary — Confidential." Paper stock: Crane Crest Ivory. The same stock used for the replica envelope.
